# Finding Balance in Remote Work

Two years into remote work. Here are some honest reflections and lessons learned.

## Discipline is Built, Not Born

When I started working remotely, I naively thought discipline was a personality trait. I learned it's something you cultivate through environment design.

My method: sit at the desk at 8 AM every day, regardless of how I feel. The fixed start time creates a ritual — a mental transition similar to commuting to an office.

## Over-communicate

In an office, you can tap a colleague on the shoulder. Remote work eliminates spontaneous interaction. My strategy: when in doubt, say more. Write detailed PR descriptions. Update progress more frequently in Slack.

## Physical Boundaries Matter

The biggest lesson: the physical boundary between work and life. It took me a year to realize that working in the bedroom was a terrible idea. Now I have a dedicated workspace — leaving that room means "clocking out."

Remote work is a double-edged sword — freedom and responsibility in equal measure. Finding your rhythm takes time, but it's absolutely worth it.
