# Thoughts on Tech and Life

It's been several years since I wrote my first line of code. The path from confusion to confidence has been challenging, but deeply rewarding.

## Why Frontend

I chose frontend development because of the instant feedback — write a line, refresh the browser, see the change. That immediate gratification hooked me.

Over time, I came to appreciate that frontend is far deeper than "painting pages." State management, performance optimization, accessibility, internationalization — each is a discipline in its own right.

## The Power of Open Source

Contributing to open source accelerated my growth more than anything else. Reading other people's code, submitting PRs, responding to code reviews — these experiences taught me lessons no course could. The beauty of open source is that you collaborate with the best developers in the world, and they don't even know your background.

## Keep Learning

Technology changes fast. My strategy: master the fundamentals deeply (JavaScript, networking, browsers), then adapt quickly to the tools built on top. Solid foundations make every new framework learnable.

It's raining outside as I write this. Sometimes slowing down to reflect matters more than always running forward.
